Track record

The record

  • 1.5°C

    I joined 1.5°C as an intern and had to step into the CTO role ten months later, when the founding engineer left. As co-founder and CTO, I lead the computer vision, satellite imagery, and fire-risk models — with the engineering, ML, and environmental science team that builds them with me.

  • Agents

    Nine AI agents in production on Hermes, open-source, on our own servers. They support different areas of the company, like engineering, sales, support, marketing, and fire alerts, and each one has a person accountable for it.

  • Beyond CTO

    I took on Customer Success, Support, and Marketing at different points and led HR for two years. I organized support ticketing and CS documentation, covered Marketing between hires, and handed each area off.

  • Open source

    Contributor since 2022 to ForeFire, CNRS's open-source wildfire simulator, maintainer since 2025, and co-author on its JOSS paper — reviewing and merging PRs, cutting releases, Dockerization, CI/CD, documentation.

  • Now

    Open to senior technical leadership or senior individual contributor roles in climate, energy, govtech, and open source — where the hard part isn't the model but putting it inside an operation where being wrong is expensive.
