About

I joined 1.5°C as an intern, became CTO ten months later, and today I'm co-founder.

It's a Brazilian climate tech company built around one idea: you can't manage a wildfire you don't see coming — prevention, response, all of it starts there.

Tech alone doesn't solve the climate crisis. It needs policy, community, and people who show up for the parts nobody claps for — that's the part I care about most.

If you're working on something in that same direction — wildfire, climate, agentic AI — I'd like to hear about it.

Pantera

A hybrid edge/cloud AI platform covering the full cycle — prevention, detection, response, impact.

17M+hectares monitored
190cameras in the field
95%detection accuracy
10mointern → CTO

It combines a network of 190 cameras across Brazil with satellite feeds and fire risk modeling to detect wildfires with 95% accuracy, taking on one of the country's largest sources of CO₂ emissions.

Some of that ground is the Pantanal and indigenous territories — the kind of land where losing a few hours of detection time actually matters. We also work with clients in pulp/paper, agriculture, and conservation to cut fire impact on the ground.

Agents

8 AI agents now run day-to-day operations at 1.5°C — without anyone babysitting them.

Alongside Pantera, I set up the infrastructure behind agents covering Sales, Customer Success, Support, and Marketing — orchestrated on Hermes, an open-source agent framework I deployed on our own servers. Each agent owns a domain.
