What it is

Where the fire
goes next

ForeFire simulates how a wildfire front moves, using real wind, terrain, and fuel data. Researchers and operational teams use it to run "what if" scenarios: where does this fire go in the next six hours if the wind shifts.

At Pantera, it's the same simulator behind our own spread forecasts — turning a confirmed detection into a projected path a brigade can act on.
