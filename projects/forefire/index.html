ForeFire

The open-source fire-spread simulator built by CNRS / Université de Corse. I've contributed since 2022 and maintained it since 2025 — on my own time, alongside Pantera.

What it is

ForeFire is a C++ engine that simulates how a wildfire front actually moves — fed by real wind fields, terrain, and fuel data, not a simplified toy model. It's used by researchers and operational teams to run "what if" scenarios: where does this fire go in the next six hours if the wind shifts. At Pantera, it's the same engine behind our own spread forecasts — turning a confirmed detection into a projected path a brigade can act on.
