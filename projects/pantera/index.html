Model decision

When in doubt, alert

Better a false alarm than a missed fire. Missing real smoke can delay the response to a wildfire, so we prioritize recall in the smoke detection model, letting as little real smoke slip through as possible, and accept the trade-off: at around 80% precision, about one in five detections is a false alarm. Camera re-checks and our 24/7 review team stand between a detection and a confirmed alert.
