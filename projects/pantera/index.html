Pantera

Cameras

Coverage

Accuracy
95% detection accuracy — measured in the field, not in a benchmark.

What it does

Detection

The camera network and computer vision models flag smoke early enough for it to still be a small fire.

Risk

Weather, fuel, and terrain layered together to flag where a fire is likely to start or spread next.

Response

Turning a confirmed detection into a coordinated action on the ground — the part that actually stops a fire.

Impact

Measuring what changed afterward — area burned, land recovered — the case for doing this at all.
