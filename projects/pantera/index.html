Why recall

Missing real smoke can delay the response to a wildfire, so here a false negative costs more than a false positive. We prioritize recall in the smoke detection model and accept the trade-off: at around 80% precision, about one in five detections is a false alarm. Camera re-checks and our 24/7 review team stand between a detection and a confirmed alert.
